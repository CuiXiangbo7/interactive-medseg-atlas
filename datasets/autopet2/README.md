## 🧾 Dataset overview

autoPET-II is a whole-body FDG PET/CT lesion-segmentation benchmark designed to study domain generalization. Its challenge page explains the clinical role of metabolic imaging and the difficulty of manually outlining lesions at scale.

The paper-entry image count is shown separately from the official release scope below. Dataset files are not mirrored in this atlas.

## 🗂️ Official release and split

The challenge page describes a training database of 1,014 whole-body FDG-PET/CT studies from 900 patients, acquired at one site. The paper-entry count of 1,014 is a study count, not a patient count.
