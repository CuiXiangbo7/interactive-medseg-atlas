## Dataset overview

CFB-GBM is a longitudinal, multi-modal glioblastoma collection with imaging, segmentation contours, treatment and follow-up information. TCIA’s current collection includes the CFB-GBM v2.0 release. Not every subject or timepoint should be assumed to contain every modality or every segmentation class; consult the collection manifest and release-specific documentation.

## Official split and label availability

TCIA does **not** define a universal train/validation/test split for this collection. The collection is released for research use, and users who construct a cohort or split should describe their inclusion criteria, modality availability, and split procedure as their own protocol.

Available segmentations and radiotherapy structures are case- and timepoint-dependent. Use TCIA’s manifests and the version-specific CFB-GBM documentation to determine which contours apply to a particular image.
