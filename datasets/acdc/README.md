## 🧾 Dataset overview

The Automated Cardiac Diagnosis Challenge uses cine cardiac MRI from the University Hospital of Dijon. Its study population covers five balanced groups: healthy controls, previous myocardial infarction, dilated cardiomyopathy, hypertrophic cardiomyopathy, and abnormal right ventricle.

The paper-entry image count is shown separately from the official release scope below. Dataset files are not mirrored in this atlas.

## 🗂️ Official release and split

The official challenge page describes 150 different patients in five equally sized clinical groups. Its training and testing pages provide the challenge-specific files; the paper-entry count of 200 is retained separately from the official patient total.
