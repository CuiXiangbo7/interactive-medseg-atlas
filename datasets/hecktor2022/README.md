## 🧾 Dataset overview

HECKTOR 2022 uses head-and-neck FDG PET/CT for primary-tumour and involved-lymph-node segmentation, with a separate outcome-prediction task. The dataset links imaging to the radiotherapy and oncology setting.

The paper-entry image count is shown separately from the official release scope below. Dataset files are not mirrored in this atlas.

## 🗂️ Official release and split

The challenge site separates the training and validation data from the held-out test evaluation. Its two prediction tasks are primary-tumour/lymph-node segmentation and recurrence-free-survival prediction; do not treat the outcome task as additional segmentation labels.
