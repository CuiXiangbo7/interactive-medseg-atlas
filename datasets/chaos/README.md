## 🧾 Dataset overview

CHAOS combines abdominal CT and MRI from separate patient groups to benchmark healthy-organ segmentation. CT annotations cover the liver; MRI annotations cover the liver, right and left kidneys, and spleen, with T1-Dual and T2-SPIR sequences.

The paper-entry image count is shown separately from the official release scope below. Dataset files are not mirrored in this atlas.

## 🏷️ Segmentation targets and labels

| Item | Dataset information |

| Label definitions | CT cases have liver masks; MRI cases have masks for liver, right kidney, left kidney, and spleen. The two modalities therefore do not share an identical label set. |

## 🗂️ Official release and split

The official Zenodo challenge record describes 20 training and 20 test cases for each CT and MRI component.
