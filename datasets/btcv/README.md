## Dataset overview

BTCV is associated with the MICCAI 2015 Multi-Atlas Labeling Beyond the Cranial Vault workshop and challenge. The abdominal challenge data contain 30 labeled CT cases for development and a separate 20-case test cohort. The labels cover 13 organs: spleen, right and left kidneys, gallbladder, esophagus, liver, stomach, aorta, inferior vena cava, portal and splenic veins, and right and left adrenal glands.

## Official split and label availability

| Cohort | Cases | Labels |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Training/development | 30 | Reference labels provided |
| Test | 20 | Test labels are withheld for challenge scoring |

The official source defines training and test cohorts; it does not establish the commonly used 24/6 or 18/12 training/validation splits as organizer-defined splits. Those are secondary experimental protocols and are intentionally not listed as official here.
