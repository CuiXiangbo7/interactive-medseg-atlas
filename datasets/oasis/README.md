## 🧾 Dataset overview

OASIS-1 is the cross-sectional release of the Open Access Series of Imaging Studies. Its official site provides structural MRI and demographic/clinical data for studies of brain structure and aging.

The paper-entry image count is shown separately from the official release scope below. Dataset files are not mirrored in this atlas.

## 🗂️ Official release and split

The OASIS-1 official page reports 416 participants aged 18–96, with three or four T1-weighted MRI scans acquired during a single session for each participant. The paper-entry count of 436 is retained as the cited image-level entry.
